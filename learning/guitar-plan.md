# Guitar practice plan

Use four practice blocks, with three 15–25 minute sessions per block. Repeat a block as needed and use the [skill tree](skill-tree.md) to record progress. A slow, steady eight bars provide a useful starting recording.

| Block | Song and lesson | What to practice | Record at the end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Clean changes | [A Horse with No Name](https://www.justinguitar.com/songs/america-a-horse-with-no-name-chords-tabs-guitar-lesson-sg-014) | Tune, isolate the two shapes, listen for muted strings and change on the first beat. Count the bars aloud. | Eight bars at 60 BPM for G1. Listen back for buzz, dropped beats and early changes. |
| 2 · Four-chord songs | [Stand By Me](https://www.justinguitar.com/songs/ben-e-king-stand-by-me-chords-tabs-guitar-lesson-bs-323) and [Porch Lights](../songs/porch-lights.md) | Learn each chord sequence from its own chart. Play quarter notes first, clap the eighth-note pattern, then transfer it to the Porch Lights verse. | Eight bars at 70 BPM for R2/G2. Mark the first missed chord entry or strum. |
| 3 · Keys and textures | [JustinGuitar Grade 2](https://www.justinguitar.com/classes/beginner-guitar-course-grade-two), modules 9 and 11 | Practice a movable shape, then try fingerpicking. Play Porch Lights in G, then with a capo at fret 2. Say the sounding chord names before playing. | G3 takes in G and A. Play a slow fingerpicked section and compare its balance to a strummed take. |
| 4 · Rehearse a full form | [Porch Lights](../songs/porch-lights.md) and the twelve-bar blues in JustinGuitar module 13 | Count in, switch grooves on the assigned bar and keep going after a slip. For a stretch, sing the Porch Lights cue over lighter strumming. | G4 full take and one transfer exercise. For the combined milestone, also log V2 and R2 evidence. |

In each session, spend the first few minutes on the change that stopped the last take. Loop four bars with a click, then add the preceding and following bars. End with one uninterrupted take at the current tempo. Listen back once, pick a specific bar for next time, and use the [practice log](../templates/practice-session.md).
