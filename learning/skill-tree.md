# Skill tree

Use the rows as a matrix. Each skill builds on the previous grade in its track unless its row names another prerequisite. The grades, tempos and accuracy targets are proposed practice benchmarks for this vault. Adjust the starting tempo to your current level and use one target at a time.

## Grading

| Score | What the evidence shows |
| --- | --- |
| 0 · Untested | No attempt logged. |
| 1 · Learning | Can demonstrate the movement or concept with pauses, prompts or a slower click. |
| 2 · Repeatable | Meets the row's pass check twice in one session. |
| 3 · Retained | Meets the same check on a second day with ordinary performance cues. |
| 4 · Transferable | Meets the same check in a new song, key, tempo or arrangement and records what changed. |

Score each skill separately. Move to the next row when the prerequisite is repeatable. Record one uninterrupted audio, video, MIDI or DAW take where applicable, and log the exact tempo and section. A stopped or restarted attempt can guide practice. [Practice session template](../templates/practice-session.md)

## Vocal

| ID | Skill and lesson | Repertoire or drill | Pass check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| V1 | Match a reference note and sustain it. Use a keyboard and the [vocal app](../projects/vocal-training.md) or a pitch monitor. | Five notes within a comfortable range | Match at least four notes and hold the middle second of each within ±35 cents using a steady tone. Score the center of the note when using vibrato. |
| V2 | Hear intervals and coordinate pitch with a click. [Ear training exercises](https://www.musictheory.net/exercises) | [Porch Lights vocal cue](../songs/porch-lights.md#melody-and-vocal-cue) | Sing all five target notes in a comfortable key with four of five correct pitches and all five entries in their intended beat slots. |
| V3 | Compare keys and keep phrasing consistent. [Notes and scales](https://learningmusic.ableton.com/) | [Porch Lights vocal cue](../songs/porch-lights.md#melody-and-vocal-cue) plus a selected song with a contrasting range | Record the cue in two viable keys. Compare range, diction, pitch and the breath plan. Choose a performance key and repeat the phrase with all five pitches. |
| V4 | Control entrances, diction and vocal dynamics while accompanying yourself. Requires R3 and G2 or K2. [Song structure](https://learningmusic.ableton.com/) | A full [Porch Lights](../songs/porch-lights.md) arrangement with verse and chorus vocal cues | Record the full form. Match the chart's section entries, execute the marked breath points and recover by the next phrase after a slip. |

## Rhythm

| ID | Skill and lesson | Repertoire or drill | Pass check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | Hear a pulse and place the backbeat. [Ableton beats](https://learningmusic.ableton.com/) | “We Will Rock You” exercise | Count 16 bars and clap on beats 2 and 4 for at least 30 of the 32 backbeats. |
| R2 | Maintain eighth-note subdivision through rests. [Ableton bars and beat playground](https://learningmusic.ableton.com/) | [Porch Lights verse pattern](../songs/porch-lights.md#two-ways-to-play-it) clapped on its own | Play eight bars at 70 BPM with no added or dropped beats and at least seven bars with the exact pattern. |
| R3 | Switch between downbeat and offbeat playing. [Ableton backbeats and genre lessons](https://learningmusic.ableton.com/) | [Porch Lights folk and reggae-inspired versions](../songs/porch-lights.md#two-ways-to-play-it) | Alternate four bars of each at 80 BPM. Both switches land on the bar line and the click stays aligned. |
| R4 | Maintain form with sparse time cues. [Ableton song structure](https://learningmusic.ableton.com/) | A practice recording with its click muted for bars 5–8 and 13–16 | Perform the full form. Rejoin the click within one eighth note at bar 9 and land the final downbeat within one eighth note of the reference. |

## Combined milestones

| Milestone | Prerequisites | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Play and sing | R2 + V2 + G2 or K2 | Two four-bar passes with pitch and chord-entry targets met simultaneously. |
| Transpose for the singer | V3 + G3 or K3 | Record both candidate keys. Match the sounding chart and confirm the vocal phrase in the selected key. |
| Change genre | R3 + G3 or K3 + M2 | Two arrangements of eight bars in the same key and tempo. Identify the rhythmic and textural changes by timestamp. |
| Deliver a set piece | R4 + G4 or K4 + V4 if singing + M4 | Full take with count-in, section cues and backing tested on the intended rehearsal device. |
